Profile

I come from a diamond manufacturing family in Thrissur. I studied computer science, then went back to stones properly — training with GIA in gemology and in jewellery manufacturing.

Today I am a partner at Jwala Diamonds & Jewellers, where I served as CEO from 2017 to 2022. The work taught me that jewellery is a business of trust measured in fractions of a millimetre: a stone is either what the paper says it is, or it is not.

The engineer in me never left. I still write code — web applications, Python, Go, cloud and data, and lately AI — and I bring the same habit to both trades: look closely, measure, write it down.

Passion

  1. A — Stones

    Diamonds & jewellery

    Natural diamonds and studded gold jewellery: grading, specification, manufacturing and quality control. I care about the unglamorous parts — the certificate, the spec card, the tag on every piece — because that is where trust is actually made.

  2. B — Systems

    Software

    Web applications, Python, Go, cloud infrastructure, data and AI. Most of what I build is a tool that removes a person from a repetitive job, or a number that lets an owner see the business from a phone.

  3. C — Image

    Design & photography

    Jewellery design, identity and the camera. A piece has to be drawn before it is made and photographed after. I do both; some of it is on Instagram.
